## **Goal**

Your task is to generate a list of **original, creative, and casual** descriptive phrases for music. The goal is to create casual phrases that sound like how a real, passionate person would describe a piece of music they just heard, using informal vocabulary rather than standardized technical terms.

## **Creative Guidelines**

* **Genre Diversity:** Your phrases should span a wide variety of musical genres, from aggressive metal and atmospheric electronic music to intimate folk and grand orchestral scores.
* **Linguistic Variety:** Mix simple, direct descriptions with more complex, figurative, and metaphorical sentences. Use a varied and informal vocabulary.
* **Evoke a Scenario:** Many descriptions should create a strong sense of place, time, or activity (e.g., "music for a road trip," "sounds like a final boss battle").
* **Embrace Colloquialisms:** Use natural, informal language and slang where appropriate (e.g., "banger," "chilled-out," "grimy," "vibe").
* **Focus on Feeling:** The primary goal is to communicate the *feeling* of the music, not to provide a technical analysis.
* **Vary Length:** Create a mix of both short, punchy phrases and longer, more detailed descriptive sentences.
